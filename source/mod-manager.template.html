Click a row to select it, Ctrl+click to add or remove one, Shift+click for a range, Ctrl+A for everything shown. Right-click for actions and tags. Disabling renames a jar to .jar.disabled. Deleting moves it to the Recycle Bin.

Snapshots

A snapshot remembers every jar in the mods folder and whether it's on or off, and keeps a copy of each jar, so you can go back even after deleting or updating mods. The app also takes one by itself before updating several mods, before restoring, and before finding a problem mod.

Disk cleanup

Old logs, crash reports and other leftovers from the game and the server. Everything goes to the Recycle Bin. Worlds, screenshots, configs and mods are never on this list.
